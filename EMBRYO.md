# Embryo Disk Health

Embryo Disk Health is Embryo-Zero's fork of [smartctl_exporter](https://github.com/prometheus-community/smartctl_exporter) (9ddc80b, commit 9ddc80b). It does hard-drive health numbers for Metrics.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
